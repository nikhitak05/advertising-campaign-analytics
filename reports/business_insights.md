# Business insights

Scope: 190 observed ad-group/month rows, 40 ad groups, July–November 2021. Monetary figures use source currency units; currency has not been independently verified. Profit here means revenue minus advertising cost, excluding other business costs.

- Overall spend was 635,372 versus revenue of 561,960: an advertising loss of 73,412. Weighted CTR was 34.56%, conversion rate 10.38%, CPA 6.62, and ROAS 0.884×.
- October was the only profitable month: 1,047 profit and 1.013× ROAS. November spend rose from 78,256 to 280,753, while revenue rose from 79,303 to 252,833; November lost 27,920. Seasonality, promotions, and changing group coverage prevent a causal interpretation.
- `Shop - 1:1 - Mob - [shop coupon]` generated the largest absolute group loss, 15,022 on 91,497 spend. Its 37.18% CTR exceeded the portfolio CTR, but its 5.24% conversion rate was below the portfolio rate. Investigate search intent, landing-page relevance, and conversion tracking before a controlled bid or budget reduction experiment.
- The highest-profit group, `Shop - Exact - Mob - Black Friday/Cyber Monday`, earned 116 on only 44 spend (3.636× ROAS, 24 conversions). This is a small seasonal sample, unsuitable for a large budget shift.
- Among groups spending at least 1,000, `Shop - Exact - Desk - Promo Code` had the highest ROAS, 0.999×, with a loss of 25 on 23,846 spend. It is a candidate for a small optimization experiment, not evidence of scalable profitability. No group meeting this spend threshold achieved ROAS above 1.

Recommendations: prioritize the largest loss contributors for tracking and relevance reviews; compare matched periods and devices; test small budget changes with a holdout and a predefined incremental-profit goal. Do not assume historical ROAS predicts marginal returns. CPA alone is insufficient when value per conversion varies.

Limitations: observational monthly aggregates, only five months, unequal group coverage (190 of 200 possible combinations), anonymized advertiser, no attribution window or customer lifetime value, unknown currency, rounded source monetary fields, and inconsistent supplied ratios. Missing combinations are absent observations, not zero performance. Revenue is kept separate from underlying sale amount. Commission-income interpretation is a project assumption pending primary data-dictionary confirmation. Revenue minus ad spend is not full company net profit.
